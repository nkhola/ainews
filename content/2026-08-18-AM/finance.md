Global bond markets experienced a significant sell-off, pushing US 10-year yields to multi-year highs and triggering a broad tech sector decline, particularly among chipmakers. This macro pressure highlights the growing cost of capital for AI investments, even as investor sentiment remains surprisingly bullish on equities. Meanwhile, geopolitical tensions and domestic political skirmishes continue to add layers of uncertainty to the economic outlook.

Broadcom also fell. JPMorgan's Kay Herr suggests the Fed should "just hike and move on" to provide clarity, while BofA's Mark Cabana attributes the bond sell-off largely to Fed policy [Fed Should ‘Just Hike and Move on With It,’ Says JPM’s Herr](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-08-18/fed-should-just-hike-and-move-on-with-it-jpm-s-herr-video), [Bond Selloff Is Mostly a Fed Story, Says BofA’s Cabana](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-08-18/bond-selloff-is-mostly-a-fed-story-says-bofa-s-cabana-video). #### Why it matters
Rising borrowing costs directly impact the valuation of growth-oriented tech stocks by increasing discount rates and making future earnings less attractive, especially for companies with high capital expenditure needs.

### Trade-offs & Evolution: Bullish Sentiment vs. Macro Reality
Despite the bond market turmoil and rising yields, fund managers remain surprisingly bullish on stocks, with a Bank of America survey indicating rarely seen optimism [Fund managers have rarely been this bullish about stocks](https://www.marketwatch.com/story/fund-manager-have-rarely-been-this-bullish-about-stocks-says-bank-of-america-survey-dccd304a?mod=mw_rss_topstories). This sentiment persists even as concerns about higher interest rates, global slowdowns, political instability, and AI capital expenditure are acknowledged. The market continues to "shrug off" rising Treasury yields, with some strategists suggesting yields need to climb much higher before a significant selloff is triggered [Stocks keep shrugging off rising Treasury yields](https://www.marketwatch.com/story/stocks-keep-shrugging-off-rising-treasury-yields-heres-the-level-that-could-finally-trigger-a-selloff-80c1634c?mod=mw_rss_topstories). This contrasts with the immediate negative reaction seen in tech and chip stocks today, suggesting a potential disconnect between broad market sentiment and sector-specific vulnerabilities. The "capex party" in AI continues, with some arguing it won't stop [Forget the bond rout, fund managers are in party mode](https://www.ft.com/content/e57f1e66-578e-42c9-8e59-37fe05067032), even as the cost of that party grows.

#### Why it matters
The divergence between strong investor sentiment and deteriorating macro conditions creates a fragile market environment, where a sudden shift in perception could lead to rapid re-pricing across asset classes.
